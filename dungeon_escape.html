📖   HOW TO PLAY

🎯 GOAL

Survive 5 floors of the dungeon and reach the exit to escape alive. Each floor is a randomly generated 5×5 grid.

🧭 MOVEMENT

Use the direction pad (↑↓←→) to move between rooms. Unknown rooms appear as ? on the map until visited.

🚪 ROOM TYPES

  • · Empty — Safe passage
  • ⚔ Monster — Turn-based fight
  • 💰 Treasure — Free gold!
  • ⚠ Trap — Takes HP damage
  • 🛒 Shop — Buy upgrades
  • 🐉 Boss — Defeat to advance
  • 🚪 Exit — Next floor

⚔️ COMBAT

Choose to Attack, Use Potion, or Flee. Each victory grants XP and Gold. Enough XP triggers a Level Up — fully restoring HP!

💾 SAVE & LOAD

Click Save at any time to store progress in your browser. Load Game from the main menu to continue your run.

📈 LEVELLING

Defeat monsters to gain XP. Each level increases ATK, DEF, and Max HP — and fully restores your health.
